Summer Programme

The Linton club will remain open on Tuesday evenings throughout the summer. The summer programme starts on 27 May and runs each Tuesday from 10 June to 19 August. It includes the Ribbands Rapidplay tournament, problem-solving, coaching, studies and occasional talks illustrated by players' games.

For the first few weeks there will be two rounds of the Ribbands Rapidplay each evening, starting at 7.30pm and 8.40pm. Tea and coffee will be available during the break, but members are encouraged to bring their own mug. Later in the summer, double-rapidplay evenings will alternate with mixed evenings featuring one round of rapidplay and another activity.
