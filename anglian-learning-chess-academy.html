Anglian Learning Chess Academy

The Anglian Learning Chess Academy is a partnership between Linton Chess Club, the Anglian Learning Trust and Sawston Village College. Our aim is to develop strong chessplayers through a combination of coaching and play.

We meet on Friday evenings in rooms S1 and S2 in the science block at Sawston Village College, from 6pm to 8pm. In the first half of 2026 the Academy runs from Friday 9 January to Friday 27 March, excluding half-term on 20 February, and from 17 April to 22 May. The Academy then closes until September.

We accept students from Year 5 upwards and the programme is free. Players who have mastered the basics develop their potential by playing increasingly strong opposition, in groups of similar ability, with small-group coaching and competitions including the Cambridge Schools League. The Academy is not suitable for beginners or those looking to learn how to play.

The Academy is run by experienced chessplayers from Linton Chess Club: Paul McMahon, Alan Richardson, James Cole, Nathan Weersing, Paolo di Fruscia, Stuart Jeffreys and Paul Kemp. Mauricio Varea runs the Schools League teams.

Further details are available through the Sawston Village College Chess Academy page. Choose Chess Academy from the list and complete the registration form at the foot of the page.
