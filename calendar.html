Calendar & Forthcoming Events 2026

We have combined the calendar and tournaments to streamline presentation for the chess club and the Chess Academy. This page contains club news and local tournaments suitable for adults and juniors. Tournaments just for junior players, including the forthcoming Megafinals in Cambridge and Peterborough, appear on the Chess Academy pages.

We no longer carry Bury League fixtures on the website because this duplicates information on the League Management System (LMS), which also carries detailed results, player records, team statistics and league tables.

Club News

The Linton Chess Club is likely to be open throughout the summer. League matches and the club championship continue to mid-May. Thereafter, stronger Ravenclaw players will be invited to take part in rapidplay tournaments, puzzle evenings and studies. At the end of August we will revert to normal club evenings as the new season starts in September.

External Tournaments

British Championships — University of Warwick, 1–9 August

The premier event in the British chess calendar offers nine days of chess for all players, from beginners to grandmasters. Rapidplay and standard-play tournaments are available. Full details are on the ECF Events Calendar.

Braintree Congress — Friday 31 July to Saturday 1 August

Two standard-play tournaments, Open and Under 1200, with six rounds and a 50+10 time control. Suitable for Ravenclaw and stronger Gryffindor players, or those rated above approximately 900.

Cambridge FIDE-rated Congress — Whittlesford, 22–23 August

Three tournaments: Under 2300, Under 1800 and Under 1550. Five rounds of standard play with a 50+30 time control. See the entry information.

Braintree Rapidplay — 29 August

Open and Under 1200 tournaments suitable for middle-ranking Gryffindor players upwards.

Cambridgeshire Rapidplay — Whittlesford, Sunday 27 September

Four tournaments: Open, Under 1900, Under 1650 and Under 1400. Five rounds with a 25+10 time control.

Bury St Edmunds Congress — 17–18 October

Cancelled because of double booking by the venue.
