Safeguarding Policy

Linton Chess Club is committed to providing a safe and welcoming environment for all players, including juniors and vulnerable adults, to enjoy chess. All club members are responsible for maintaining high standards of courtesy, sportsmanship and conduct. Suspected inappropriate behaviour should be reported to the club's safeguarding lead.

The club has a number of officers with enhanced DBS certification. They are experienced players, and most have coached junior players or organised competitions over many years.

The highest safeguarding priority is ensuring that no child or vulnerable adult comes to harm at the club. Harm includes deliberate abuse, bullying, neglect and inappropriate or offensive comments. Anyone suffering abuse, or anyone seeing or suspecting abuse, should report it to a safeguarding official or the club duty officer. Incidents will be recorded and investigated, with external agencies contacted where necessary.

Junior players should be registered on arrival and departure. Juniors under 14 should be accompanied by a parent or responsible adult, who should remain on site until the child is settled and confident. Emergency contact details must be provided.

Junior players selected for away matches should normally be transported by their parents. Parental approval is required if a junior travels with another team member. Photographs of children may only be taken and used with parental permission.
