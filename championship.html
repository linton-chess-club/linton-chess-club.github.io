Linton Club Championship Competition

The Linton club championship is held each season as a five-round Swiss tournament. The championship dates from 1999, with the Jim Carr Memorial Shield presented annually to the winner. Since 2019, a second trophy, the John Dawson Memorial Shield, has been awarded to the highest-placed player in the lower half of the draw.

The 2024/25 championship saw Konrad Scheffler score 5/5 to take the Jim Carr trophy, while Nate Weersing retained the John Dawson trophy as the highest-placed player from the lower half of the draw.

Paul Kemp and Nate Weersing, winners of the Jim Carr and John Dawson trophies in the 2022/23 season. Nate retained the John Dawson trophy in 2023/24, with James Cole taking the Jim Carr trophy as overall winner.
